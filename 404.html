Frequently Asked Questions

Are my documents uploaded to a remote server?
No. DocBuilder processes all files 100% locally on your computer or phone using in-browser WebAssembly and Canvas APIs. Your files never leave your device memory.
Is DocBuilder completely free to use?
Yes, DocBuilder is 100% free with unlimited conversions, exports, and document editing. There are no subscriptions, paywalls, or account registrations.
Can DocBuilder work offline?
Yes. Once loaded, DocBuilder operates entirely in client-side memory without needing an active internet connection.
What is the maximum file size supported?
Since processing occurs in device RAM, DocBuilder comfortably handles documents and image batches up to several hundred megabytes depending on your available device memory.
