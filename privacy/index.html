Privacy Policy & Security Architecture

Your Documents Never Leave Your Device. Read our technical privacy architecture and on-device data guarantee.

Key Features & Privacy Highlights

  • Zero Cloud Uploads: Documents are decoded and transformed entirely in local memory
  • No Persistent Storage: Files exist only in volatile RAM during your active session
  • Zero Third-Party Trackers: No Google Analytics, no telemetry, no tracking pixels
  • Zero Cookies: We do not set identifying cookies or profiling sessions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you store copies of documents or photos?
No. We do not operate file conversion servers or databases. When you close your browser tab, all memory is instantly purged by the browser engine.
Does DocBuilder make external network requests when opening files?
No. All font rendering, character maps (cmaps), WebAssembly modules, and icon assets are hosted locally on our static origin.
