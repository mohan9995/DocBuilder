Frequently Asked Questions

Are my signatures stored anywhere?
No. Signatures are drawn onto an in-memory canvas and baked directly into the PDF binary. They are never transmitted over any network.
What is the Help (i) icon feature?
It drops an information badge onto the document. In Adobe Acrobat, Apple Preview, or Chrome PDF reader, clicking the badge pops open the note containing your text.
Can I undo my changes?
Yes. Doc Studio provides undo and redo controls so you can safely iterate on annotations and page modifications.
