Merge PDF Files Online – Fast, Private & Free

Combine multiple contracts, reports, or invoices into one clean PDF document. All merging runs in browser memory on your device.

How to Use Merge PDF Files Online – Fast, Private & Free

  1. Upload PDFs: Select or drag two or more PDF files from your computer or phone.
  2. Arrange Order: Use the up/down controls or drag handles to organize the sequence of documents.
  3. Combine & Download: Click "Merge Pages" to compile and save your merged PDF instantly.

Key Features & Privacy Highlights

  • Zero server uploads — 100% confidential and private
  • Flexible page sequencing and ordering
  • Instant in-memory compilation via pdf-lib
  • No file quantity limits or watermarks added

Supported Formats

Supported Inputs: PDF (.pdf)

Supported Outputs: Combined PDF (.pdf)

Technical notes: Encrypted/password-protected PDFs must be unlocked before merging

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I reorder PDFs before combining them?
Yes. You can reorder files up and down to define the exact sequence of pages in the output document.
Are merged PDFs stored on any server?
Never. All page copying and document assembly occurs entirely in your browser memory using WebAssembly.
Is there a limit on how many PDFs I can merge?
No. You can merge as many PDF files as your local device RAM permits.
