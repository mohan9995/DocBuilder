Frequently Asked Questions

How does DocBuilder compress documents on-device?
DocBuilder re-samples and re-encodes embedded image streams and page geometries using optimized canvas compression buffers in memory.
Will my document quality degrade?
You have full control. At 80-85% quality, documents look virtually identical to the original while reducing size significantly.
